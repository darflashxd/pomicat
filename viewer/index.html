What this page does and does not protect
  • Decrypted in your browser
    AES-256-GCM. The key is in the link's #fragment, which browsers do not send to the server. Anyone holding the full link can read this page.
  • Not indexable
    Crawlers that see the link without the #fragment only get ciphertext. Links can expire, burn after one read, or be revoked by the sender.
  • Copy deterrence (not protection)
    Glyph scrambling, an invisible watermark and a DevTools check make casual copying harder. A screenshot defeats all of them: once text is on screen, the reader can keep it.
