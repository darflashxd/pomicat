How private Word sharing will work
  1. Connect Microsoft and capture the saved document.
  2. Review redactions in a formatted preview.
  3. Share an encrypted link to the reviewed copy.

Recipients will read the copy, with optional DOCX export. Editing in Word will require a separate copy in their Microsoft account.
