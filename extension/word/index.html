Private sharing for AI chats and documents

Stop crawlers from reading
your shared chats.

Shared AI chats have turned up in Google search. PomiCat hides what is private before you share, then sends an encrypted link whose key never reaches the server, so search engines and crawlers get nothing they can read.

Share an AI chat

ChatGPT · Claude · Gemini · DeepSeek

  1. Open the conversation and click the PomiCat button in the bottom-right corner.
  2. Compare the original with what others will see. Click a highlight to hide or restore it, or select text to hide it yourself.
  3. Create a private link. Set an expiry, burn it after one read, or revoke it later.

Share a document

Word Online · Google Docs · Experimental integration

  1. Open a saved .docx in Word Online, or a Google Doc, and click the PomiCat button in the bottom-right corner.
  2. PomiCat reads a separate copy on your computer. Your original stays as it is.
  3. Download the cleaned DOCX or create an encrypted link that expires or can be revoked.

What gets checked

  • Names and placesFound by a small model that runs in your browser.
  • IDs, accounts and credentialsNational IDs, bank accounts, cards, phone numbers, passwords and API keys.
  • Amounts and the maths behind themA salary, plus the "20% is $1,000" line that would let someone work it back out.
  • Tracked changes and commentsRemoved from Word copies by default, so old wording does not travel with them.
  • Document detailsAuthor, company and other hidden Word properties are cleared unless you keep them.
  • ImagesMark areas by hand in Word copies. The pixels are replaced, not covered with a shape.
Why crawlers get nothing
  • The key is the part of the link after #. Browsers and crawlers never send it to a server.
  • The relay stores only ciphertext and tells search engines not to index it. Opening a link needs a click.
  • Nothing leaves your computer until you create a link. Detection can miss things, so read the copy before you share.
What the recipient gets

Recipients read the cleaned copy in their browser and can download it as DOCX. To edit it in Word, they save their own copy to their Microsoft account.
